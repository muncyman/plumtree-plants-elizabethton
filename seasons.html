🌲 From the Mountains to Your Garden

Many of our flowers, greenhouse vegetable plants and herbs come from our grower's operation in the mountains of western North Carolina, near Grassy Creek and Spruce Pine. Seasonal trees come from the mountain tree farm. Plants are brought to our Elizabethton location as inventory is needed throughout the season.

Questions about a specific plant? Contact or visit us to check current availability.
