Helpful growing tips

Plant care made simple.

Quick pointers for some of our most popular seasonal products. Check plant tags and ask our team for variety-specific recommendations.

🌼 Caring for Mums

Place in bright light, keep soil evenly moist without waterlogging, and remove spent blooms to keep plants looking fresh.

🌱 Perennials

Choose a planting spot suited to the variety’s sun and soil needs. Water deeply while plants establish roots.

🎃 Keeping Pumpkins Fresh

Keep pumpkins dry and sheltered from excessive heat. Avoid storing them directly on soggy ground.

🎄 Fresh-Cut Christmas Trees

Place the trunk in water promptly and check the reservoir daily. Keep trees away from heat sources and open flames.
