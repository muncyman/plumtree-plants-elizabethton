Grow with confidence

Plant care made simple.

Helpful reminders for the plants and greenery you bring home.

🌼 Mums

Keep soil evenly moist and give plants plenty of sun. Check containers daily in warm, dry weather.

🎃 Pumpkins

Place on a dry surface and avoid prolonged wet conditions to help prevent premature rot.

🎄 Cut Christmas Trees

Use a water-holding stand, check the water level daily and keep trees away from heat sources.
